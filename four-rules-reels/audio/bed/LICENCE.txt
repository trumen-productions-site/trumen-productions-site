MUSIC BED — LICENCE
===================

The bed in these renders is not licensed third-party music. It is
synthesised at render time by src/audio.py:synth_bed() — summed detuned
harmonics with a slow swell and a one-pole low-pass filter.

It is therefore an original work owned by Revelatory Productions, LLC,
with no third-party rights attached and nothing to clear.

It is also a PLACEHOLDER. It exists so the turn has lift and so the
ducking and loudness stages have something real to act on. Replace it
with a scored or licensed bed before publication, and record the licence
here when you do.
